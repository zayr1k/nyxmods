## What is it

Nyx Mods is a game platform for Windows: it finds the games and launchers you already have, manages their mods and modpacks, and connects you with friends. It doesn't replace your launchers — hit «Play» and the right one opens with the right version.

**Minecraft: Java Edition is the first supported game** (official launcher, Legacy Launcher, TLauncher, Prism and more). **More games are on the way** — Nyx is built so new games plug into the same mods, modpacks, feed and community.

## Features

### 🎮 Games & mods
> Available now for Minecraft; the same tools come to every new game.

- **Auto-detects launchers** and every installed version — no account logins, no reading your passwords.
- **One-click mod management**: enable, disable, drag & drop to add, delete to the Recycle Bin. Nyx warns you when a mod doesn't match your loader (Forge / Fabric / NeoForge / Quilt).
- **Modrinth catalog** filtered to your version: installs with dependencies, every file verified by hash.
- **Download Minecraft versions** from Mojang's servers — Java is installed along with the game.

### 📦 Modpacks
- Ready-made Modrinth modpacks, `.mrpack` import, «Save my mods».
- **Turn a modpack on — your own mods are safely set aside. Turn it off — they're back.** Nothing gets lost.

### 👥 Community
- **Friends by username** and real-time **direct messages**.
- **Drop mods and modpacks right into the chat** — your friend installs them with one button.
- **Modpack feed**: publish yours, like, install others' in one click.
- **Forum** with sections and threaded replies.
- Avatar and «About me».

### ⚙️ Comfort
- Lives in the **system tray** — close the window, downloads keep going.
- **Starts with Windows** (can be turned off).
- **Auto-updates** on launch, just like Discord.
- Dark minimal interface, English and Russian.

## Installation

1. Download [**Nyx-Mods-Setup.exe**](https://github.com/zayr1k/nyxmods/releases/latest) from the latest release.
2. Run it — one-click install, no admin rights needed.
3. If Windows shows **«Windows protected your PC»**, click **«More info» → «Run anyway»**. This happens with new apps that don't have a paid code-signing certificate yet.

After that, Nyx keeps itself up to date.

## Security

- **No user files, ever.** Only links to Modrinth mods can be shared in the feed and chat — the files themselves are downloaded from Modrinth and verified by hash. You can't pass a virus through Nyx.
- **Avatars are images only**: Nyx redraws them at 256×256 on your PC; your original file is never uploaded.
- **Links on the forum are removed automatically** — on the server.
- Nyx **never reads your launchers' passwords or tokens** and doesn't bypass licensing — you play through your own launcher as usual.
- **Only friends can message you**; there are reports and anti-spam limits.
- The app is sandboxed (strict CSP, integrity checks); updates are downloaded only from GitHub and verified by SHA-512.

## FAQ

<details>
<summary><b>Do I need an account?</b></summary>

Not for mods, modpacks or playing. A Nyx account (email + password + username) is only needed for friends, chat, the feed and the forum. It isn't linked to Minecraft or Microsoft.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Where are my mods when a modpack is on?</b></summary>

Safe: Nyx moved them aside and puts them back as soon as you turn the modpack off.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>How do I fully quit Nyx?</b></summary>

Right-click the icon near the clock → «Quit Nyx». Or turn off «Close to tray» in Settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Which games are supported?</b></summary>

Minecraft: Java Edition is the first one. Nyx is a multi-game platform and new games are coming in the next updates — tell us which ones you want in **Forum → General**.
</details>
